# Split SYCL queue core

**Order:** 14
**Priority:** P1 — moves queue lifecycle and regular execution onto the device linkage established by the blocker
**Blocked by:** `06-sycl-device`
**Source:** `docs/changes/005-split-big-files/spec.md`

## Outcome

Split the queue lifecycle, submission plumbing, regular copy execution, completion, and queue factory out of `src/sycl/copy.cpp` without changing the SYCL backend contract. The resulting private queue linkage keeps `SyclQueue`, its task and sequence-outcome state, and fence state visible exactly where required, while `src/sycl/copy.cpp` remains the owner of fault/fence/launch support, host transfers, testing counters/hooks, and a temporary out-of-class `SyclQueue::execute_binary(Task&)` definition for task 15. The in-order asynchronous queue, fixed resource reservations, registry/quarantine behavior, metadata slots, context, no-op handling, completion proof and failure retention, and allocation seams remain unchanged.

## Scope

- Add private `src/sycl/queue_support.hpp` for the queue-local fence declarations and linkage needed by the split. Preserve the existing `SyclFenceState` completion state, including the locking and repeatable behavior of `mark_completion_proven()` and `completion_proven()`; do not create a second fence state or alter fence result/failure retention.
- Add private `src/sycl/queue_internal.hpp` for the declarations of `SyclQueue`, `Task`, and the SYCL sequence-outcome state. The outcome declarations must retain `detail::WorkspaceLease` and all existing owner-entry, binary-entry, fence-state, and sequence fields required by the existing completion path. Declare a private `execute_binary(Task&)` member as the responsibility boundary used by the regular `execute(Task&)` dispatcher; keep these declarations private and preserve one-definition-rule behavior.
- Add `src/sycl/queue.cpp` for the moved queue lifecycle and submission path, the complete `execute(Task&)` dispatcher and regular copy branch, completion, the fault-checked native queue factory, public `make_queue`, and the testing resource snapshot implementation. The binary branch dispatches to `execute_binary(Task&)`, whose temporary definition remains in `copy.cpp` until task 15. The moved code must use the device linkage established by `06-sycl-device` rather than introducing another device or resource owner.
- Retain in `src/sycl/copy.cpp` the submission-fault state and consumer, fence/fault/launch support, `launch_scatter_words`, `launch_gather_words`, and `launch_view_transfer`; retain `region_from_host`/`region_to_host`, the fence-wait counter hooks, fault injection/reset hooks, and retained-queue-lease testing hook. Include the private queue headers as needed without duplicating definitions.
- Move the current `SyclQueue` declaration and lifecycle definitions from the lifecycle/support area (current `copy.cpp:42-420`) into the private declarations and `queue.cpp` while leaving the support definitions that serve host transfers in `copy.cpp`.
- Move the queue submission block (current `copy.cpp:421-463`) into `queue.cpp`. This includes regular copy submission and binary submission plumbing only; it must not move binary staging arithmetic or binary execution.
- Refactor current `SyclQueue::execute(Task&)` (`copy.cpp:698-997`) without splitting one member definition across translation units: move the complete dispatcher and regular non-binary branch to `queue.cpp`; move the current binary branch unchanged into a private out-of-class `SyclQueue::execute_binary(Task&)` definition that remains temporarily in `copy.cpp` for task 15. Move `SyclQueue::complete_task` (`copy.cpp:999-1040`) to `queue.cpp`.
- Move `make_queue_with_fault_check`, `make_queue`, and `queue_resource_snapshot_for_testing` (factory/snapshot anchors at current `copy.cpp:1043-1083`, plus the existing public factory definition) into `queue.cpp`. Keep `inject_submission_fault_for_testing`, fence wait counter functions, and host-transfer/testing support in `copy.cpp` (current `copy.cpp:1101-1187` and the retained hooks immediately before it).
- Update the SYCL target in `CMakeLists.txt` to compile `src/sycl/queue.cpp` and the two private queue headers while retaining `src/sycl/copy.cpp`; do not change test target source lists or public factory headers. `src/sycl/add.cpp` is untouched.
- Keep `src/sycl/queue.cpp`, `src/sycl/queue_support.hpp`, and `src/sycl/queue_internal.hpp` at no more than 499 physical lines after normal formatting, with the stricter queue.cpp target of 470. The intentionally retained `copy.cpp` binary helper may remain oversized in this intermediate task; task 15 removes it and enforces the final `copy.cpp <=420` and universal production-file cap.

## Implementation references

- **Modify:** `src/sycl/copy.cpp` — current `iom::sycl_detail` fault state, `SyclFenceState`/fence and launch support, host transfer functions, testing fault/counter hooks, and retained-lease hook. Remove only the queue-core definitions assigned to `queue.cpp`; leave binary arithmetic/execution and all retained support intact.
- **Add:** `src/sycl/queue_support.hpp` — private fence declarations and the shared fence-state linkage that preserves `mark_completion_proven()`/`completion_proven()` semantics.
- **Add:** `src/sycl/queue_internal.hpp` — private `SyclQueue`, `Task`, and sequence-outcome declarations, including `detail::WorkspaceLease` and the private `execute_binary(Task&)` declaration.
- **Add:** `src/sycl/queue.cpp` — `SyclQueue` lifecycle, copy/binary submission plumbing, the `execute(Task&)` dispatcher and regular copy branch, completion, fault-checked queue construction, `make_queue`, and `queue_resource_snapshot_for_testing`.
- **Modify:** `CMakeLists.txt` — add the queue translation unit and private queue headers to `iom_sycl`, retaining the existing SYCL copy source and excluding no retained source.
- **Do not modify:** `src/sycl/device.cpp`, `src/sycl/add.cpp`, public SYCL headers, tests, or any other backend. Device declarations/resources and their linkage are supplied by the blocker task.

## Requirements

- Preserve every public header and signature, including `Device::create_ops()` and `sycl_detail::make_queue`; this is a source-factoring change, not an API or capability change. Do not add compatibility aliases, overloads, shims, backend switches, global registries, or cross-backend abstractions.
- Define every moved symbol exactly once. Private declarations may be included by both SYCL translation units, but no non-inline definition may be emitted in both; preserve anonymous/private visibility and existing ODR behavior.
- Preserve the existing in-order `sycl::queue`, `detail::StagedWorker<Task>` callbacks, submission mutex ordering, sequence/token allocation, outcome map, and callback completion flow. Queue construction must reserve queue resources before native queue creation and worker publication, and all construction rollback behavior must remain transactional.
- Preserve registry registration/removal, invalidation and quarantine semantics for source/destination owners and binary entries. Retain `detail::WorkspaceLease` in sequence outcomes and release it only through the existing completion-proof path; never release an unresolved lease merely because a callback or wait returned.
- Preserve fixed metadata-slot ownership and slot protection/release rules. A successful covering drain may release protected slots; uncertain completion must retain/quarantine the original queue partition and associated resources. No metadata growth, replacement, extra native allocation, or changed allocation seam is permitted.
- Preserve context identity and native queue construction, including `sycl::property::queue::in_order{}` and the `SubmissionFault::queue_stream_create` injection point. Fault categories, synchronous-vs-tokenized failure behavior, validation order, and retained exception identity must remain unchanged.
- Preserve regular copy behavior, including identical-window no-op handling, task/fence creation, metadata upload and kernel launch, post-launch failure retention, completion proof, owner-entry release/invalidation, and repeatable `wait` outcomes. A no-op must continue to avoid native work and fence state as before while retaining its sequence semantics.
- Keep binary arithmetic, host staging arithmetic, and binary execution in the temporary `SyclQueue::execute_binary(Task&)` definition in `src/sycl/copy.cpp` for task 15. Queue submission plumbing and the dispatcher may capture/enqueue/route binary tasks, but this task must not change binary algorithms, workspace offsets, broadcast mapping, dtype support, numerical behavior, or binary device allocation behavior.
- Keep host transfer paths (`region_from_host`, `region_to_host`, `launch_view_transfer`) and their staging leases, transfer waits, fault behavior, counters, and testing hooks in `copy.cpp`. Do not redesign synchronization or transfer ownership.
- Keep `src/sycl/queue.cpp` at most 470 lines and each new private header at most 499 lines after normal formatting. Task 15 must leave `src/sycl/copy.cpp` at most 420 lines and every touched/new production file at most 499 after moving the temporary binary helper; do not treat the binary-retaining intermediate `copy.cpp` size as a task-14 failure.
- Do not add tests or alter test source lists. Existing SYCL smoke, conformance, coexistence, and configured remote targets are the future behavior proof; enabled hardware tests must fail rather than skip.

## Non-goals

- Do not modify `src/sycl/device.cpp` or implement any part of the device split, device tensor ownership, device arena/resource setup, or blocker linkage beyond consuming its established private declarations.
- Do not move, implement, or redesign binary staging arithmetic or binary execution; those responsibilities remain in the temporary `SyclQueue::execute_binary(Task&)` definition in `copy.cpp` and move to `src/sycl/queue_binary.cpp` in task 15. Do not touch `src/sycl/add.cpp`.
- Do not change public SYCL headers, `DeviceOps` signatures, queue capabilities, error categories, validation order, token encoding, queue limits, context selection, allocator behavior, synchronization, or cross-queue ordering.
- Do not introduce a new fence implementation, completion protocol, workspace/registry tracker, global state, backend-neutral queue abstraction, compatibility path, or synchronization redesign.
- Do not migrate host transfers, staging-pool ownership, fault/counter APIs, or testing hooks out of `copy.cpp`; do not add tests, CMake test sources, or a permanent physical-line-count test.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/sycl/queue_support.hpp`, `src/sycl/queue_internal.hpp`, and `src/sycl/queue.cpp` exist at the planned private paths and contain the exact queue-core responsibilities above; `src/sycl/copy.cpp` still contains retained support, host-transfer, testing, and the temporary `execute_binary` definition.
- [ ] Every queue-core declaration, lifecycle/submission member, `execute(Task&)` dispatcher, regular copy branch, completion member, factory, and testing-snapshot definition is present exactly once. `execute_binary(Task&)` is declared once and temporarily defined once in `copy.cpp`; `detail::WorkspaceLease` remains in outcome state with no ODR or private-visibility regression.
- [ ] The queue uses the blocker-provided device linkage while preserving resource reservation-before-publication, rollback, in-order asynchronous execution, submission ordering, registry/quarantine behavior, metadata-slot protection, context identity, no-op behavior, allocation seams, completion proof, and repeatable success/failure outcomes.
- [ ] Fence completion state retains the existing `mark_completion_proven()` and `completion_proven()` locking and result semantics; uncertain completion continues to retain resources and failures rather than releasing or replacing them prematurely.
- [ ] Binary arithmetic/execution is unchanged in its temporary helper, while device implementation, public headers, `src/sycl/add.cpp`, tests, and test source lists are unchanged. Queue submission plumbing and dispatch do not alter binary behavior.
- [ ] The SYCL CMake target includes `src/sycl/queue.cpp`, `src/sycl/queue_support.hpp`, and `src/sycl/queue_internal.hpp` alongside retained `src/sycl/copy.cpp`, without changing public factory headers or test source lists.
- [ ] `src/sycl/queue.cpp` is at most 470 physical lines and both private headers are at most 499 after formatting. Task 15 owns the final `src/sycl/copy.cpp <=420` and universal touched-file cap after removing the temporary binary definition.
